05 Documentation

Transfer Learning for Time Series Forecasting

This platform uses pretrained N-HiTS and N-BEATS models trained on the M4 competition dataset. These models learn universal time-series patterns and can be applied zero-shot to new domains.

Setting Fine-Tuning Steps > 0 adapts the pretrained weights to your specific data using a few gradient steps, improving accuracy at the cost of additional computation time.

Zero-Shot Inference

When Fine-Tuning Steps = 0, the model runs in pure transfer-learning mode — no training on your data. This is the fastest option and works well when your data shares characteristics with M4 training data.
